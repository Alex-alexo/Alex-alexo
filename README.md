📄 Publications

1. Multimodal Based Amharic Fake News Detection Using CNN and
Attention-Based BiLSTM

Published in Scientific Reports (Springer Nature).

The research developed a multimodal model combining:

CNN-based image processing

Attention-based BiLSTM for text

Late fusion of textual and visual information

FastText word embeddings

Image preprocessing using grayscale conversion, CLAHE, and median
filtering

The study used 23,856 Facebook news posts containing text and images
and reported 98% accuracy.

DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-17579-w
